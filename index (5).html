WhatsApp and Excel were built for general use, not for running a travel agency. When you're managing 20+ active cases, the cracks start showing — messages get buried, deadlines get missed, staff don't know what to do next. TravelDesk keeps everything organized automatically so you don't have to think about it.
How long does it take to get started? +
Most agencies are fully up and running within one day. We handle the setup for you — account configuration, data migration, and a short training session for your team. You don't need any technical knowledge.
Will my staff actually use it? +
We include a free onboarding session with your team — either in person in Kuwait or via Zoom. TravelDesk is designed to be as simple as WhatsApp. Most staff are comfortable using it within their first day.
Is our data safe? +
Yes. All data is encrypted and automatically backed up. Ironically, your current setup — documents on personal WhatsApp, spreadsheets on one laptop — is much more vulnerable. TravelDesk gives you a secure, backed-up home for all your agency data.
Can I try it before paying? +
Yes. You get 14 days completely free — no credit card required. We also offer a free demo where we walk you through everything live and answer all your questions. Book it via WhatsApp in under a minute.
Does it work in Arabic? +
Yes, fully. TravelDesk has a complete Arabic interface and was designed from the ground up to support Arabic-first workflows. Switch between Arabic and English at any time.
